What to include
    

  • When & where: accurate timestamps and location help responders correlate related incidents and spot campaigns.
    • 

  • Indicators: URLs, IPs, sender addresses, phone numbers, hashes and wallet addresses let others block the same attacker. Defang links (hxxp, [.]) when possible.
    • 

  • Impact: money lost, data exposed and systems affected set priority.
    • 

  • Never share passwords, full card numbers or PINs in a report.


Protect yourself now
    

  • Stop contact with the scammer; do not send more money or codes.
    • 

  • Call your bank to freeze or recall transfers (speed matters most).
    • 

  • Change passwords and enable multi-factor authentication, starting with email.
    • 

  • Disconnect infected devices from the network but keep them powered for forensics if advised.
    • 

  • Preserve emails, messages, logs and screenshots as evidence.
